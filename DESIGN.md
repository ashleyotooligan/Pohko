# The November folio

Pohko is designed as a page worth returning to: warm paper, aubergine type, restrained violet fields, coral annotations and a little archivist creature. It avoids a market-terminal visual hierarchy. Entry reasons get the largest type; sources and conditions remain beside them.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | #f5f1e8 | Background |
| Ink | #29232d | Main text and receipt field |
| Violet | #715294 | Memory, support and journal controls |
| Lilac | #e6dded | Highlighted reasons and operator panel |
| Coral | #ca583e | Broken conditions and annotations |
| Green | #3b7063 | Supported conditions and price index |

DejaVu Serif provides the editorial headings in prepared figures; Sans and Sans Mono handle observations and identifiers. Browser font fallbacks may differ from the native capture environment. Exact numbers come from state, not artwork.

The full-body lilac mascot has a stepped snout, orange feet and a three-tab tail. It is original project artwork, independent of any provider identity. Its square image is used in the README, cover and journal masthead. See [provenance](PROVENANCE.md).

Twelve layouts share a page margin and folio mark but use different compositions: index cards, a large entry sheet, condition columns, an observation ledger, paired charts, a source comparison, a version spread, freshness bars, digest rows, an operator brief, a receipt and a queue.
